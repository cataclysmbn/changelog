Bright Nights changelog: 2026-10-19 ~ 2026-10-25

Oct 19

Remove the test message shown when the grenade hack self-destructs



Add more monster kinds to subways and sewers

• Sewers get insects, spiders, centipedes, slugs, raccoons, and others. Subways rarely get hell creatures and mutants.



Add oil rig on lakes

• You can get fuel and industrial supplies there. It can also be chosen as a start location for the Pond Scum scenario.



Combat engineer starting gear changes to a shotgun and an ammo bag

• The hatchet and bolt cutter go in the MOLLE pack, and pistol skill is also added.



Add money tree and knowledge tree to Crazy Cataclysm



Grab follow-up attacks trigger only when the attacking monster is holding you itself

• Fix being hit by a finishing attack of an unrelated monster while grabbed by another zombie.



Breaking doors at close range with shot is easy again

• Shot terrain damage is counted 5 times, so wooden doors break reliably.

• Shot does not get hit penalties for small targets, or for crouching or prone.



Oct 20

Show defense and dexterity penalties in the chimera skin mutation description



Muzzle attachments can no longer be fitted to revolving rifles



Casings of 6.8mm, 40mm, and 25mm can be disassembled

• 6.8mm casings give brass. Other casings give scrap metal.



Monsters can grab each other too



RM99 revolver capacity 5 rounds -> 6 rounds, and muzzle attachments can be fitted

• The damage bonus drops from +10 to +5, and burst fire is added to the automatic pistol.



Fix whip attacks and monster attacks passing through floors while clairvoyant



Interior vehicle doors no longer get locks

• If you hotwire the vehicle and open the door, you can remove the door lock without removing the security system.



Sunken cargo ship and underwater lab spawn better in lakes close to cities

• Terrain sonar detection range 7 -> 10 tiles



Monster ranged pulling reflects grab strength

• The 3-tile maximum stays, and it cannot pull you across floors.



Oct 21

Add jet skier profession to the Pond Scum scenario

• You start in swimwear with a jet ski.



8x40mm and 20x66mm caseless ammo can be disassembled



Auto-travel to remembered stairs is possible

• If you move with `<` or `>`, you actually go up or down the stairs.



With marksmanship below 4, recoil control in sustained fire is slower

• The per-gun beginner penalty is gone from level 2 of that firearm skill.



Military special locations are more varied

• 2 kinds of outpost each appear with 25% chance. Camps, bunkers, helipads, and others appear one per overmap with 35% chance.

• Prison spawn rate is halved.



Add colored light to lit flares, glow sticks, and torches

• Glow sticks glow green and flares glow dark red. This also applies to Arcana gear and the sparkledog fursuit.



Add more kinds of clothes from zombies

• Hoodies, leggings, and slippers drop more often, and tour t-shirts, halter tops, and high heels can also be obtained.



Oct 22

Arcana summoning sigils are shown in the arcane materials tab



Fewer empty rooms in house basements

• Empty basements more often get small rooms and supplies.



Add new drinks cheap armor drink and horchata



Add slippery clown zombie and sticky clown zombie to Crazy Cataclysm

• They spit lubricant and glue.



Overhaul the body armor system

• Light, normal, and heavy ballistic vests and ballistic plates are worn separately.

• Plates block with 85% chance. ESAPI plates are light and also stop rifle rounds, but break easily after a few hits.

• Add scrap metal ballistic plates that you can craft early.



Oct 23

Old Guard ballistic vest can also take plates

• The old plate-reinforced recipe is removed, and full arm and leg ballistic protection is not provided.



You can disassemble 13 bionics, such as blood filter, to recover parts



Strengthen mr skeltal in Crazy Cataclysm

• It gets dedicated tiles for the trumpet, top hat, and red eyes, and has health, defense, and melee damage on the level of a skeletal brute.



Capacity of pistol and rifle ammo belts and flintlock pouch is unified to 50 rounds



Add rider tiles to hauler bot and some Arcana vehicles

• The flying polyp tile position is also adjusted so that the real position is easy to see.



Oct 24

No comfort message while reading

• It is shown only when you try to sleep or when limb health is not full.



Monsters can also use superhuman dodge

• Feral black ops use superhuman dodge instead of small-target dodge.



More forest monster spawns, lower swamp monster density

• Normal forest: 15% chance of 1-3 -> 20% chance of 1-4. Swamp: up to 6 -> 4.



Liquid connection needs 2 pipes and 1 rubber or leather hose

• This uses fewer hoses than the old 2 rubber hoses.



Ammonia crafting does not use scrap metal



Add a paddle to the second seat of canoes and rowboats

• When several people row, the strength of each rider counts toward the thrust.
