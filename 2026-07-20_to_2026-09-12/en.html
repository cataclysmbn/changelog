Bright Nights changelog: 2026-07-20 ~ 2026-09-12

Jul 20



Replace the tiles of the MBR vest, military exo-suit, and industrial exo-suit

• You can now tell the two exo-suits apart by color and shape.



Reload or unload a placed turret without picking it up

• Examine the turret to see its remaining ammo.



Armor plates can now be put into and removed from the old Guard IOTV suit

• It is based on the empty MBR vest and can hold magazines. It needs 12 armor plates.



Add Hunter, Detective, and Archaeologist choices to the time anomaly scenario

• The Detective and Archaeologist start with gear that fits the period, such as a silver watch instead of a smartphone.



Cargo carriers can now be disassembled

• The normal type gives 80 lumps of steel and 20 short ropes. The carbon fiber type gives 6 carbon fiber frames and 20 short ropes.



Add tabs by All, Favorites, Other, and magic school to the spell selection window

• Favorites and the last tab you used are saved. You can search when you switch tabs.



Fix ceiling lights not spawning in wood-floor rooms of labs



Jul 21

Add the missing workshop toolbox to the vehicle survival workbench and robot workbench



Add missing tool functions to Aftershock craft furniture. Vehicle kitchen units can now purify water



Combination guns can now be found more often in antique and hunting gun storage places and similar locations

• BB guns and paintball guns also spawn in toy stores, sporting goods stores, and similar places.



Add the M6 survival gun and the .410 caliber conversion tool

• You can convert the pipe shotgun, double-barrel pipe shotgun, and 12 gauge pistol to use .410 ammo.



Choose starting spells and spell levels with points in character creation

• You can choose only spells that the scenario and profession allow.



Long spell descriptions can now be scrolled. Fix the description overlapping hotkeys



Read Arcana pattern scrolls like spellbooks to learn spells

• Remove the 10x difficulty modifier that made spell learning take longer.



Jul 22

Fix per-item spawn multipliers not applying to map generation

Jul 27

Cutting, stabbing, and bullet damage that goes through armor and is above 15 now causes bleeding

• Enemies immune to bleeding are excluded. Monsters also lose health from bleeding.

• Wait one turn to press on the wound and stop the bleeding. The higher your first aid skill, the shorter the bleeding lasts.



Adjust the layout of downtown shop areas and apartments that spawned awkwardly at the edge of the city

• Parking towers and office towers spawn more often.



Fix the timer of a time bomb to run correctly and explode while you wait



Aluminum items can now be cut with a hacksaw to recover materials



Fix errors or empty dirt floors when you load a save that has old apartments



Fix the number of items placed on the map not matching the spawn multiplier



Arcana gilded aegis healing now also stops bleeding

Fix some robots not becoming hostile when attacked
