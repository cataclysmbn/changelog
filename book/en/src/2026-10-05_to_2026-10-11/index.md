# Bright Nights changelog: 2026-10-05 ~ 2026-10-11

Oct 05

## No warning when you enter a slippery tile while wearing non-slip gear.

## Fix bug where stat modifiers from poison, hallucination, artifacts, and similar effects stacked repeatedly.

## Add missing range bonus to submachine guns and pistol-caliber rifles.

- Mostly machine pistol +3, small and improvised submachine gun +5, normal submachine gun +10, pistol-caliber carbine +20.

- Colt Ring Lever Rifle has damage +8 and range +4. Colt Lightning .45 carbine and Henry Big Boy .44 have range +10.

- Magnum Research BFR damage penalty changes from -15 to -8, with a range penalty of -12.

- Bond Arms Derringer and Taurus Judge have damage -4 and range -2. Mini Draco has range -3.

- Shotguns also get a range modifier of about half their damage modifier.

## +10 base range for 4.6x30mm and 5.7x28mm in the Exotic Ammo Types mod.

- Pistols that use this ammo get a separate range penalty.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/52986f0e27d5800675a8a4e3fe4b6749df5bf1844003408f2bd571e649da1f8d.webp)

## Redraw the wielded tile of the M1014 shotgun.

## Fix bug where some lab rooms removed the required stairs.

- Stairs now also spawn on floors other than concrete, such as linoleum. Locked rooms, isolation rooms, and spots with furniture or traps are excluded.

## Tools that use light batteries also accept disposable and nuclear types of ultra-light batteries.

- For example, put an ultra-light plutonium fuel cell in a tactical headset, or use it in a hotplate with the light battery mod.

Oct 06

## Enemy grab status also ends when the enemy and the player move apart.

## Trait requirements in the RPG System mod use the higher of the current stat and the base stat.

- Base stats include invested points. You no longer need to delay a trait because pain lowered a stat.

## Remove photosensitivity and carnivore from the lizard mutation line.

## Adjust ranged bionic weapons.

- EMP Projector: damage 10 -> 20, armor penetration 4 -> 9, power use 50 -> 25.

- Chain Lightning: damage 16 -> 25, range 10 -> 12, power use 100 -> 50.

- fusion blaster: damage 22 -> 60, power use 50 -> 100. Firing noise also increases.

- Power shot: damage 22 -> 20. It uses an area explosion that deals heat damage, not one that sets fires.

- Add fusion blaster rifle and its recipe to the base game. Power shot can also be fitted to the rifle.

## Add firearms and ammo.

- Add .36 caliber revolving rifle and smokeless powder rounds. Colt M1861 Navy can also be crafted.

- Revolving rifle crafting difficulty is 1 higher than for pistols. Also fix spawning with more ammo than capacity.

- Add M1919 machine gun and .30-06 ammo belt and links. It can be modified to fire from the shoulder.

- Add SVD and PKM, and 7.62x54mmR ammo belt and links.

- Belt feed adapter can also be fitted to .30-06 and 7.62x51mm firearms.

- Add technicals with M1919 or PKM mounted. Technical fuel changes to diesel.

- You can also find military surplus firearms in museums.

## Adjust black powder rounds and .410 shot.

- .44 paper cartridge: damage 30 -> 28, armor penetration 7 -> 6.

- .36 paper cartridge: damage 25 -> 21, armor penetration 5 -> 4. Smokeless powder rounds are adjusted too.

- .410 shot: total damage 30 -> 50, base range 20 -> 16.

## Add .410 purified shot to Arcana.

- It can also be loaded into Taurus Judge and Bond Arms Derringer to use as a sidearm.

## Add AR-57 that uses 5.7x28mm to the Exotic Ammo Types mod.

Oct 07

## Built-in toolset can also repair bronze, brass, platinum, and superalloy gear.

- Modifications that increase the size of metal clothing, such as arc welder, are also possible.

## Raise armor of armored zombie to match heavy power armor.

- Small-caliber firearms now have difficulty dealing damage.

## Lower the mutagen toxicity of Shiny! floor wax and dessert topping to 5 per serving.

- A bug made it 10 times that of one serving of mutant meat. A cooked mutant meat chunk is 24.

## Fix building display errors in the UndeadPeople tileset.

- Fix map display breaking with the rotation of the red apartment, and remove yellow corners on some floors.

- Also fix the A letter on the yellow apartment roof and the vertical plant garden display.

## Tiles covered with plasma count as dangerous terrain, and you catch fire if you stand on them.

- A plasma tile itself does not set terrain on fire.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/ffbfa2d7f3a0d0e3826f9d8219a82b55e6895a9ba695802fd382cec1b95efe56.webp)

## Adjust firepower and consumption of plasma rifle and anti-materiel cannon.

- Plasma ammo: damage 35 -> 40, armor penetration 8 -> 10, range 12 -> 20.

- XM-P plasma rifle: gun damage 5 -> 10, UPS use 5 -> 10. Remove the fire effect and reduce explosion size.

- PPA-5 plasma cannon: gun damage 22 -> 15 with a 3x damage multiplier. UPS use 100 -> 15, but each shot uses 3 ammo.

- Anti-materiel cannon armor penetration 60 -> 30, and the explosion is larger. Neither weapon pierces obstacles.

- Reload times are also swapped, so the anti-materiel cannon takes longer.

## Add IFAK. It mainly drops instead of the first aid kit from soldier zombie, police zombie, and others.

- Contents: 3 bandages, 3 medical gauze, medical adhesive tape, 4 doses of powder disinfectant, 3 doses of hemostatic agent.

- The soldier zombie first aid kit and the police zombie compact first aid kit remain at low chance. Remove the separate hemostatic agent drop from soldier zombie.

## Fix small holes in the long hair tile.

Oct 08

## Fix bug where the revive effect applied to the player when an NPC died in the revival mod.

## Add delivery drone that you unfold on the floor and fly remotely.

- It unfolds as a 1-tile vehicle with a full battery.

## The screen and view follow a remote-controlled vehicle.

- The camera turns on and off automatically when control starts and ends. When you end control or the link breaks, the view returns to the player.

- Instead of the old 40-tile limit, you can control vehicles within the currently loaded map range.

- When you end control, the engine of a stopped ground vehicle turns off. Vehicles that are moving or in the air stay on.

- Vehicle info and the heading marker are shown during remote control too.

## The drone delivery profession starts with a smartphone, and the barter and conversation skills are removed.

- Pizza delivery driver's driving skill 4 -> 2.

## Higher chance to get farsighted glasses and bifocals from zombies.

- For a normal zombie, the two types together go from about 0.17% to 3.75%. Glasses and watches can also drop damaged.

- Glasses can also appear in wardrobes and office desks. Zombie pockets and bags hold more kinds of items.

## Add Plumber profession. Delinquent teen starts with a super soaker.

## Pipes can store and supply many kinds of liquid, not only water.

- Set the liquid for each tank and pipe fixture. Other liquids such as gasoline can be stored separately in the same pipe.

- Faucets and tanks that handle several liquids are also supported.

- Adding a liquid setting uses a pipe kit and rubber hose. Removing it returns them.

## Vehicle battery charging station charges for the elapsed time during waiting and crafting too.

- Fix waiting 5 minutes adding only 1 to 2 charge. In a test it charged 180.

## You can fill a box with ammo using the reload function.

## Bullet vibrator (on) turns off automatically when fatigue is above 384.

- This prevents dying of exhaustion after falling asleep with it on from a UPS.

- Fatigue still builds up too fast while waiting or sleeping.

## Add armor-piercing and black powder rounds to 7.62x54mmR ammo.

- The existing ammo is renamed FMJ. Crafting and disassembly recipes are also added.

## Higher chance to get CBMs from cyborgs and similar enemies.

- broken cyborg: 50% -> about 66%. More kinds of CBMs can drop.

- Lab mutants: 20% -> about 33%.

- The CBM chance of zombie scientist and the power storage chance of civilian corpses are set to 50%.

## When you throw a zombie, the thrown zombie also takes more collision damage.

- The damage to the thrown one is multiplied by 6. Both the thrown monster and the monster it hits apply only half of their bash armor.

- Stamina cost goes from 40 to 100 per distance, and the maximum cost from 800 to 1500.

## When loading images are on, progress is shown in a small 3-line window at the bottom of the screen.

- Less text covers the center, so the picture is easier to see. When images are off, the old display stays.

## Less ammo from common military vehicle turrets.

- 5.56 and 7.62 mm: 10-100 rounds -> 3-30 rounds. .50 caliber: 10-100 rounds -> 1-10 rounds.

- Grenades, autocannon shells, missiles, and rare vehicles such as technicals and helicopters are excluded.

## Add plate armor for horses with crafting and disassembly recipes.

- It has the same protection as superalloy barding, but is much heavier. The crafting cost of superalloy barding also increases.

## Max health and melee ability that animals gain from training now apply correctly.

- Fix trained dogs healing only to their pre-training max health. Training also applies to special attacks.
