# Bright Nights changelog: 2026-09-13 ~ 2026-09-29

Sep 13

## You can now choose Tail Fin (-2 points) and Thick Tail (1 point) in character creation

## Fix the game freezing, or the shelter rooftop door not opening, when you finish the Dino Dave mission of the Dinosaur mod

## Add a caliber conversion kit that changes the H&K UMP to .40 S&W and 9mm

- You can find the 30-round magazine and the conversion kit as loot. UMP45 magazine volume 750 -> 500 mL.

## Fix the tiles of military helmets, tactical headsets, and SWAT-line enemies

- Change the headset to a thinner olive color and give SWAT members black boots.

Sep 14

## Zombies that spot a player in a tree now hit the tree to try to knock the player down

- Climbing dangerous terrain such as a tree needs stamina. If you fail to hold on or are unlucky, you fall down.

## Monster bleeding damage now concentrates in a short time

- Damage per bleeding hit 1 -> 3, and damage checks happen 4 times as often. Duration is about 1/15 of before.

## Fix an error when you save or quit while an NPC is reading a book

## Reptile hides needed to craft lizard mutagen and serpent mutagen 3 -> 1

Sep 15

## In the simplified nutrition mod, calcium tablets, bone meal tablets, intravenous vitamin B, intravenous iron, and similar items no longer spawn

## Add a health bonus to multivitamins and gummy vitamins (5 and 4 respectively)

- It also applies in the simplified nutrition mod.

## Adjust the damage of throwing spears

- Iron javelin throwing bash/stab damage 5/17 -> 6/25. Throwing sticks now also have throwing damage 5.
- Remove the extra damage modifier that atlatl darts got when thrown by hand.

## Maximum morale from playing games 60 -> 20, maximum duration 2 hours -> 3 hours

## Frail, Slow Deterioration, and Disintegration mutations make you lose health faster while awake

- Natural recovery during sleep is also adjusted to 90%, 80%, and 70% of normal, respectively.

Sep 16

## Fix nearby monsters disappearing when you save and load in an underground place and similar locations

## Special events at the start location are now also marked as auto notes on the map from the start

## Fix zombies stumbling twice in one turn and moving as if they teleport

## Fix an NPC wandering around and picking up items after you finish the starting NPC medicine mission

## You can now use a vehicle shower in a flying vehicle

## Random character generation no longer picks animal ear and tail traits. You can still choose them when you make a character yourself

## Fix a new save format being wrongly detected as old after you reset the world after death

Sep 17

## Change the plastic material of some hats, bags, and costumes to synthetic fiber

- The materials used for repair and disassembly also change.

## Spray can capacity 10 uses -> 20 uses (250 mL), empty can weight 340 -> 250 g

- Paint and cans are easier to get in shops, and crafting paint needs extra butane.

## Block dimension travel in the old save format, and show a message to move to the new format

## The plumbing toolkit recipe is now learned automatically at mechanics 3

## You can now build a utility shelf directly

- Fabrication 2, 1 hour. It needs 8 pipes, 6 small metal sheets, and 12 plastic chunks.

Sep 18

## Fix grid-connected arc welders and built-in toolsets being used correctly for tasks that need a welding tool

## Artifacts with magic effects can now be recharged by their original charging method again

## Increase the share of lumber yards, landscaping stores, warehouses, and car dealerships in small towns

## Move the blacksite underground from floor 1 to floor 2 so surface explosions affect it less

## Jet thruster durability 120 -> 350, install, remove, and repair time 1 hour -> 3 hours

- The VTOL thruster also takes 3 hours. The hover repulsor needs mechanics 10 and 4 hours to install and remove.

## Butter, sauerkraut, and soy sauce can now replace the seasoning added to some foods

- Chili powder and curry powder can also be used for crafts that need a mild seasoning.

## Bird Wings cost 2 -> 3 points, Bat Wings and Butterfly Wings -1 -> 1 point

## Fix the game closing because of sound processing when the processing range of the surrounding area changes

Sep 19

## You can now tell a companion to dissect all corpses in an area at once

- If the precision cutting tools needed are nearby, the companion brings and uses them.

## Crafting the jet thruster, VTOL thruster, and hover repulsor is now harder

- Craft time 4 -> 12 hours, 8 -> 12 hours, and 8 -> 18 hours respectively. They need extra skills such as electronics and advanced materials.

## Add the pair of crocs

- Switching to the sport mode with the heel strap on changes encumbrance 20 -> 5 and coverage 55% -> 60%. You lose the fancy clothing check in exchange.

## Fix a modified multi cooker also pulling in a recipe that uses water purification tablets instead of the recipe that boils water

## Fix searchlight light fading too fast to be seen on screen

Sep 20

## When you turn on Arcana and a related mod together, compatibility content now applies automatically

- You no longer need to choose a separate compatibility mod when you use them with the Dinosaur mod, Magical Nights, Aftershock, and others.

## Add the Contaminant Particle Ionizer CBM

- It removes dangerous gases such as smoke and toxic gas within 5 tiles. It uses 5 kJ when turned on and 5 kJ each turn while running.
- Bionic Firefighters start with it installed.

## Fix smoke bombs, tear gas grenades, and sterilizing and insecticide gas grenades being reused again and again

Sep 29

## Add the camouflage zombie, which cannot be seen from far away, and its evolved form, the camouflaged hunter zombie

- The distance at which you spot it with normal sight is half of perception + survival (rounded down). Special vision such as infrared can see through the camouflage.
- They join the evolution line of the soldier zombie, and you also have a chance to get camouflage clothing.

## Mutations that change stats now apply to base stats

- The Alpha line of Prime Strength, Prime Dexterity, Prime Intelligence, and Prime Perception makes the matching base stat 1.5 times. A base of 8 becomes 12.
- They no longer amplify temporary stat bonuses, and the double intelligence effect of Distributed Neurology stays.

- With the default r, rr if there is one ammo type, and rrr if there are several types, selects the last ammo you used.
