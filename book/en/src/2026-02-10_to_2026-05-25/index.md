# Bright Nights changelog: 2026-02-10 ~ 2026-05-25

This period is long, so only the large changes are listed.

Feb

## Water plumbing system added

- Connect water tanks, sinks, and pipes at your base to store and draw water.

- Added a `plumbing kit` for plumbing work. You can craft it and it can spawn.

## Fruit and nut bushes can be dug up and replanted

## Autodrive works while an airplane is on the ground

- Fixed a bug that always blocked autodrive for vehicles with wings.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/41e5b0e0d27e4aee950f9dd78ba751bb0d2754c6270917e5c14b54fd8e72ccf3.webp)

## Passive power generation restored for the small and improved reactor CBMs

- The Atomic Battery CBM no longer has a radiation problem. You can upgrade it to a reactor CBM.

## Underground survivor scenarios added

- Start in a suspicious basement, chemical basement, hidden lab, meth lab, preper basement, and more.

## Dehydrating changed

- Hang the food on a drying rack and do other work. Collect the finished product later. You do not have to wait and watch.

## Good tools now affect crafting and construction speed

## NPCs can help power muscle-powered vehicles

- Passengers add power in vehicles such as a Tandem bicycle and a bicycle blimp.

## Carbon fiber vehicle parts added

- Wings, boards, roofs, aisles, cargo plates, and more.

## Heavy armored robots appear sooner

- Tank drone, chicken walker, and tripod appear in military robot groups from 2 weeks after the start.

## Monster and NPC processing performance improved

- Fewer far-away monsters are processed. Some monster movement, vehicle movement, and item processing now use multiple threads.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/70f835f2bd60719563693b73c82eae53d90a2cf72268e51a0ae2dee5531b4a8e.webp)

## Repair UI improved

- The item selection screen now shows if you have the materials, success rate, damage chance, and required tools and skills.

## Plate armor costs less to craft and covers more

- Plain, decorated, and superalloy plate armor now have 100% coverage.

## Night vision color setting added

## RM13 night vision function moved to a scout vision flag

- Active power armor helmets also support scout vision.

Mar

## Map and reality bubble structure reworked

- Added the base for a dynamic reality bubble size.

- Added multiple dimensions, pocket dimensions, and per-dimension map storage.

- Asynchronous map generation and lazy loading reduce stutter while you move.

## More fixes for stutter while moving on the map

- Improved multithreaded processing of border map loading, fire loading, and map generation.

## Fixed a bug that saved to disk each time a submap unloaded

- This reduces the large lag when you cross a map border.

## Plutonium batteries now charge for the elapsed time outside the reality bubble and in storage

## Added an option to use the old save path on Android

- Devices that have a permission problem with the Documents path can use the old save location.

## Nested categories added to the crafting menu

- Food, weapon, and part recipes are split into finer subcategories.

## Construction menu reworked

- Added tabs, categories, remaining craft count, speed modifier, and source display.

- You can mark fill, outline, circle, and ignore areas in a construction blueprint.

## Combat mount training added

- Trained mounts such as horses do not panic and run away in combat.

## Wearable gun support added

- Fire a worn gun without holding it. If you wear several guns, a selection menu shows.

## Most of the tank mod merged into the main game

- Added tank treads, large cannons, and tank vehicles, ammo, and parts.

## Cargo plane, passenger plane, and bicycle plane added

- Added an airport start location.

## Aiming UI improved

- Hostile targets are now aimed at first.

## Look around and extended info menus improved

- Terrain and furniture colors, move cost, lighting, and tile description are easier to read.

## Vehicle wing mirrors no longer collide, and a flat trunk added

## Gun recoil now also applies to vehicles

## Added craftable and installable household furniture and parts, such as canned goods, refrigerators, freezers, and multi-cookers

## Many lightweight lab, house, and city building variants added

## Many performance improvements

- Less stutter during movement, lighting calculation, and save and load.

Apr

## Jet engine, VTOL thruster, and hover repulsor added

- Added the base for crafting hover vehicles and jets.

## F-35, F-15E, Harrier, hover tank, and hover APC added

- Added the GAU-12 25mm cannon, dropped bombs, bomb bays, and handling for high-speed aircraft.

## Military airfield added

- It has 4 jets, up to 2 flying vehicles, a fuel depot, and a bomb and shell depot.

## Military blacksite added

- A secret military garage with 3 underground floors.

- The reward is 2 experimental vehicles.

## Blacksite boss fight added

- A feral black ops squad appears: a commander, a bio operator, an engineer, and a sniper.

## New boss added to the power armor room of the military outpost

- Defeat the boss to get an advanced power armor cargo frame.

## Flying monster AI improved

- Birds and flying enemies can flee to the floor above or keep a preferred altitude.

## Vehicle NBC sealed parts added

- Added parts and recipes to craft and modify CBRN-protected vehicles.

## You can give crafting jobs to NPCs

- Order a follower to start a new craft or continue an interrupted craft.

## Lab rewards and room layouts improved

- Added a chemical storage room and replaced some weak finale rewards.

- Added the experimental weapons range, dissection room, and superalloy nanoforge finales.

## Many lab bosses added

- Shaded figure, radiant triffid, alpha mutant, medical mutant, and more.

- Some bosses are linked to samples, dedicated weapons, and finale rewards.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/31518bb4c8e78c04038653676e12b58ef48bfb87a5230285cb2815ceda06f889.webp)

## Arcana and Magic Items return as built-in mods

- The related patch mods are also included.

## State police station added

- A 2-story police station by the road. You can get police gear and vehicles.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/399eb94a140e51f87c7c8d32c68cc2e9f79272a7311f1c5a94b77f67e41d7b94.webp)

## Outlet mall added

- A large shopping terrain outside the city. It has many shops, restaurants, parks, and parking lots.

## Mutation, boss, and late-game combat improved

- Added the radiant triffid, radiant blade, shaded figure, feral black ops, and more.

## Paint function added for vehicles, buildings, and items

- Use spray paint to change the color of vehicle parts, furniture, and some items.

## Added inspecting and picking locked doors and safes with the integrated toolset

## Skill books added

- Added books to learn melee and archery skills up to level 6.

May

## Trans Coast Logistics Center added

- Added a large research and logistics facility and an escape scenario.

## Start location is now near a quest hub such as Hub01 or the refugee center

- This reduces the problem of quest NPCs far away at the edge of the map where you cannot see them.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/791c5efb968923c87460580adc83045eb5d8286c8fd9d2a1a60bbb36307fd2c4.webp)

## Pick up everything nearby shortcut added

- Pick up floor and vehicle cargo from your tile and the 8 tiles around you in one menu.

## Pet training bonus added

- Training level gives HP, melee, and dodge bonuses. You can see them in the examine menu.

## Follower crafting improved

- Improved assigning crafting jobs to NPCs and making them continue the work.

## M7 rifle, 6.8x51mm ammo, and M250 LMG added

## Skills Through Kills mod added

- A Lua mod that turns off normal skill training. Skills increase with kill XP.

## Vehicle fluid converter added

- A vehicle part that does automatic work such as water purification and liquid conversion.

## Vehicle and terrain paint function finished

- You can paint furniture and items, not only vehicle parts.

## You can disassemble a nuclear reactor core to recover parts

## Many vehicle color palettes added

- More color variety for police, construction, farm, fire, emergency, military, aircraft, and wooden vehicles.

## Superalloy forge lab finale added

- Use the nanoforge to change plate metal and nanomaterial into superalloy plates.

## Experimental weapons range lab finale added

- The reward includes plasma guns, EMP projectors, railguns, and ammo.

## First quest of the relief doctor reworked

- You now look for a medical supply drop point, not a far-away hospital.

## Wasp nest spawn chance halved

- Added a wasp-removal NPC and an acidic wasp queen quest at the refugee center.

## Labs are smaller, and some room and finale rewards improved

- Not for the central lab. Normal labs are less tiring to explore.

## Ice lab rewards improved

- Added a dedicated mutagen storage finale and a locked room.

## New enemies and bosses added

- Shadow Stinger, alpha mutant, medical mutant, radiant triffid, feral black ops, and more.

## New locations and map extras added

- Crashed aircraft, dead survivors, besieged vehicles, HAZMAT response vehicles, camper vans, biplanes, and more.

## UI and control convenience improved

- Vehicle autodrive speed setting, vehicle follow distance setting, scrolling in the camera photo menu, and a larger examine menu.

- Fixed many input problems with drop, number selection, and filters.

## Many crafting, combat, and flight bugs fixed

- Power shortage while crafting, wait menu during flight, lag when flying at a high Z-level, stuck arrows, vehicle freezer food freshness, and more.
