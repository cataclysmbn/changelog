# Bright Nights changelog: 2026-06-22 ~ 2026-06-28

## Jun 22

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/5aa27032ce34edbc2987efdc6e31ba19b688f254e7acdccb945c5135887b1d6f.webp)

### Add enhanced variants of the deranged heavy-armored bionic special forces at the military outpost

- After 2 weeks, melee and shotgun types spawn. After 6 weeks, grenadier and fusion blaster types spawn.
- Their looks differ, so you can tell which weapon they carry.

### Fix errors in construction speed and skill bonus effects

- The construction speed bonus now also applies to vehicle work.
- Also fix missing calculations for the cutting skill and the see-through bonus.

### Gun-type items no longer always cover the torso

- A gun briefcase is no longer treated as torso gear.

## Jun 23

### Fix hallucinations affecting real objects

- Hallucinated monsters and NPCs no longer open doors, push cars, create fields, board vehicles, or consume items.

### Fix vision staying blocked after you remove a foldable opaque vehicle part

- When you fold or smash a part such as a cloth board, you can see right behind it.

### Craftable guns with a duplicate for each caliber now use a base gun plus a conversion kit

- Applies to the pipe rifle, handmade carbine, Lutie SMG, and more.
- Add craftable gun mods and caliber conversion kits for them.

### Add tiles for the 40mm grenade casing and the 40x53mm conversion kit

- The Kevlar vest tile also changes to a look without pouches.

### Fix a long freeze after playing with a pet

### More flour spawns

- A box is no longer fixed at 10 items. It is a random 5 to 40.
- New flour boxes in grocery stores and cargo crates are always full.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/c703af444bcb5cf15c10bb08b9a8e253ec6a594bcc35069c700ba62553919380.webp)

### The creepy doll tile now has a sulky face

### Fix airplanes losing speed right after takeoff

- Speed is kept after takeoff, and climbing and descending work correctly.

## Jun 24

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/23b71e91882abaa91d395e5f28d4e9467b46f70ed3e0dfda00d5ef9af7383035.webp)

### Add a door-opening lever inside the police station evidence room

- If you pick the police station start in the "Besieged" scenario, you are no longer trapped inside.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/eca8d5340f66997b3297599a6471517777f6576c532e1fab18ad5d64c735e402.webp)

### Mouse auto-move now works on remembered tiles

- A path line shows, and you can move, to tiles that you cannot see now but saw before.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@7a87dd98733a433d2822b7b8fcb2730a2073462a/6840a8bb5876dc0eddf93361843c3afa37bd920e9f06b23f76300976715353ee.png)

### The repair menu now shows the needed material count in advance

- The list and the repair window show the available materials and the needed count together.

### Fix skill experience in other worlds becoming 0 after you turn on the Skills Through Kills mod once

### The survivor backpack becomes a survivor MOLLE pack

- You can attach holsters to it, like a MOLLE pack.

### Add the subway start scenario "The Metro"

- You start alone in a city subway tunnel. It gives 2 points.

### Fix heating and cooling features setting a wrong temperature when you have a body temperature mutation

### Adjust rare ammo and gun spawns

- 7.62x39mm ammo appears very rarely in police evidence lockers.
- Survivor zombies carry a rare gun with about 9% chance, and veteran survivor zombies with about 25% chance.
- .50 BMG is removed from the rare ammo list, and the Draco joins the rare rifles.

### Fix wrong stairs appearing in the refugee center

### Fix infinite plastic from disassembling funnels

- A plastic funnel now weighs 150 g, equal to 3 crafting ingredients.

## Jun 25

### Fix hair being lost when you change the scenario in character creation

- The selected hairstyle and color are kept.

### Fix food in an electric freezer being frozen but also rotten after you leave the map

- Food on the floor still rots, but food in the freezer stays fresh.

### Reduce slowdown of long actions such as reading and crafting caused by an active bionic scanner

- In one report, reading time went from 76 s to about 22 s.

### Labs appear more often

- Ice lab chance rises from 1/3 to 1/2.
- Normal labs can also appear 0 to 3 times per overmap.

### Reduce the loaded volume of many guns

- Pistols, SMGs, AR types, belt-fed guns, and more get less bulky with a magazine inserted.

### Sounds from visible places no longer show a sound marker

- Only sounds from places you cannot see show on the map.

### Adjust weight and volume of some pistols to match holsters

- Applies to the Glock 20/21/22, S&W Model 10, USP, Taurus Judge, and more.

### Add the RM360 to rare SMG spawns

- You can see it rarely at gun shops and from survivor zombies.

### Add a stock slot to the Mosin-Nagant rifle

- You can cut the barrel and stock to make an obrez.

### The refugee center battery charger works correctly from the first time it is generated

### Vehicles that overlap during map generation are treated as wrecks again

### Pressing the wait key after aiming is done now waits correctly

### Necropolis and Tacoma Commune now generate only one per world

### Magic sounds are reduced overall

- Fix a bug where even small spells made sound 190.
- The spell sound cap is lowered to 150, and the value that damage adds to sound is reduced to 1/3.

## Jun 26

### Weight and volume reduction from gun mods now takes effect

- Parts that make a gun smaller, such as a folding stock, now work correctly.
- Even with too many mods, gun weight and volume never go below 1% of the original.

### Remove the base joining question that could not progress from Pablo Nunez's dialogue in the refugee center

### Fix an error in Chris Isherwood's Rescue Barry quest

- The target floor information of the Migo scout tower is now correct, so the quest starts with no error.

### Fix a pipe rifle recipe error in old saves

- Previously learned .22/.308 pipe rifle recipes no longer cause a load error.
- The new Lutie SMG recipe also works correctly.

### Rice and pasta now come in larger amounts

- Spaghetti, macaroni, and lasagna come in a default stack of 8, about 500 g.
- Rice comes in a default stack of 12.
- In restaurants and houses, the count is a random 1 to 8.

### Opaque furniture such as air conditioners now blocks vision correctly

### You can use crafting materials from inside a vehicle that is in the air

- Materials on airship seats and floors show in the crafting list.

## Jun 27

### Fix the damage log missing after a ranged attack

- After you fire a gun, "N damage" and death messages show again.

### Fix the eat and use menu cursor so it stays on the stack you just used

- Even if the same cotton ball exists on the floor and in your inventory, the cursor points to the one you just used.

### Enemies now appear at the soldier corpse scene

- Up to 2 zombie soldiers, up to 2 deranged soldiers, and 1 zombie corporal can appear.
- In other cases, a TALON UGV may remain.

### Remove the 10000 L limit on vehicle cargo and container capacity

- Storage larger than 10000 L is no longer cut off and can be used.

### Add the M249-equipped TALON UGV

- It is inaccurate at long range but fires fast, and it is very dangerous up close.
- Police robots now leave a police robot chassis with no superalloy.

### Fix some vehicle part tiles disappearing

- Part images such as the advanced 3D printer show again.

### Slightly raise heating and cooling performance

- The default stabilization value rises to 1250.
- The power armor helmet no longer has heating and cooling, and the body heating and cooling rises to 2000.

### You can throw an item of the same kind as one you wear

- Even while you wear a backpack, you can throw another backpack you hold in your hand.
