## [Expand tameable wild animals](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN/pull/10846)

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cataclysmbn/changelog@b7d8dbc30c082bb96935f77a426f3450b46e0803/d5c9ff7c3936ee706f23ea07e2b44ff4b3c503e1e34a3edc5193d8ab06d1e96c.webp)

Nov 12

## [Adjust forest animal spawns](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN/pull/10861)

Reduce the conditions that made forest animals disappear after early game, and simplify spawns by time of day.

Bear and crow cubs spawn in spring, fawns spawn in spring and summer, and some boar, dog, and cat spawns become young animals.

Wolves spawn in groups of 1~3 all day, and some animals do not spawn right after game start.

## [Fix lake and river saving and save lag](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN/pull/10851)

Fix an issue where newly generated lakes and rivers could be missing from saves.

Also fix severe lag while saving.

## [Improve The Tacoma Commune greenhouse](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN/pull/10867)
