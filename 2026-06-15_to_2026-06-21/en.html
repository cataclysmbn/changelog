Bright Nights changelog: 2026-06-15 ~ 2026-06-21

Jun 15

Fix a crash on the next turn when the reactor CBM safe overload is on and no plutonium is available



Faster EMP blast processing

• Tiles with nothing to affect are skipped, so EMP grenades and blasts cost less processing.



Change the power of the M72 LAW anti-tank rocket

• It is now weaker than the 84mm HEDP, to match the explosive balance rework.



Fix the missing carbon fiber 5.56 magazine tile



Arcana: fix some Magus Hunter and Old Guard dialogue

• Fix reactions after Sophia's third mission, book return dialogue, reputation handling, and more.



Fix a bug in the experimental pickup menu filter

• Selected items hidden by the filter are now picked up correctly.



Fix `b:` conditions being cut off wrongly in the filter and autopickup



Add disassembly recipes for Hellfire missiles

• ATGM and RPG ammo can also give rocket fuel when disassembled.



Jun 16

The vehicle laser cannon tile now rotates with its facing



Fix a whole stack being traded when you select only part of overlapping items in the trade window



Fix pets losing training and bond when they leave the map and come back

• Also fix the descriptions of the dog whistle and the examine text of combat mount pets.



Rework the kraken boss

• The kraken body now summons tentacles, instead of single-tile monsters.

• Tentacle attacks such as wrap are now used by the tentacles themselves, and the kraken and tentacle tiles and descriptions change to match.



Jun 17

Add default included mods to the experimental build

• Sky Island, squirrel mod, Otopack sound pack, and Transformers.



Add clam and fried clam tiles



Adjust ice labs appearing too often

• Make it a unique special terrain and lower its spawn chance.

• Also fix the missing State Police Station tile.



Krakens no longer appear randomly in rivers

• They now spawn only at the lake shipwreck special location.



Remove the `house basement lab` start location from the Underground Dweller scenario

• Fix an error at start caused by the old underground lab start location.



Fix autodrive being canceled at once even on a clearly visible road



CRT Expansion: fix a loading problem caused by an old enchantment value



The default first aid kit has its instructions again



Jun 18



You can read in the dark with night vision goggles

• Basic light-amplifying goggles count as weak lighting. Improved, scout, and CBM night vision count as good lighting.



Rename first aid kits

• `large first aid kit` -> `first aid kit`

• `small first aid kit` -> `compact first aid kit`



Change the decoy vehicle at the military blacksite

• Replace the old decoy vehicle with an M60 tank that has no weapons, engine, or controls.

• Add one more near the elevator to reduce the deathmobile exploit.



Less lag when crafting, reading, or passing turns near a large mobile base

• Vehicle cargo charging targets are no longer searched again every turn.



Fix a self-targeted spell showing a cancel confirmation in the middle of casting



Fix the save before sleeping option so it runs correctly after you choose the alarm or sleep time



Jun 19

Add a `no sound pack` archive to the experimental desktop downloads

• Sound stays on. It is a separate file that only leaves out the default included sound pack.



Jun 20

Fix earplugs and tactical headsets so they block the screecher's stun effect



Magic: fix targeted spells that could not be cast at visible enemies

• It now works for targets seen by normal sight, infrared, and special senses.



Fix charcoal water purifiers that could not be examined or recharged
