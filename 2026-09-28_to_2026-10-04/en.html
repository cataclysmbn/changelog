Bright Nights changelog: 2026-09-28 ~ 2026-10-04

Sep 28

Add disguised zombie

• It is invisible beyond half the distance of perception + survival. Special vision such as thermal sight can find it.

• It joins soldier zombie evolution. Kill it to get camouflage clothing.



Change how alpha mutations scale stats

• Base health is 8. With the best health mutation, health becomes 12.



In the reload list, pick the wielded weapon with the reload key

• Press twice with one ammo type, or three times with several, to load the last used ammo.



Sep 29



Spilled liquid spreads as a puddle on the floor

• Drinks, oils, and chemicals cover the floor and spread over time.

• Lubricant makes you fall. Glue holds your feet. Non-slip shoes and overshoes are also added.

• You can choose how much to pour from a container, so you can make a gasoline trail.



Close the exploit of burning disposable batteries to fill rechargeable batteries



No color selection window when the item to craft has a fixed paint color



No overeating warning for characters with hypermetabolism



Add a setting to increase the stat update interval during waiting and long actions

• Stat values update a little later, but waiting is faster.



Sep 30

Add sanding quality to angle grinder and grinder blade



Fix very fast crafting going to negative progress and stopping



Add a beginner penalty: with firearm skill 0, spread doubles

• The penalty is gone from firearm skill 1 for that gun, or marksmanship 4.

• Close-range aiming is much faster from firearm skill 6 for that gun, or marksmanship 8.



Make glue a liquid: pour it from containers and spray it on the floor

• Very rarely, a vehicle with a large amount of glue spawns.



Push or drag some electric furniture again, such as refrigerators and heaters

• Small furniture needs 1 strength. Large furniture needs 5 to 10.



Weather is applied every 24 turns, not every 5 minutes

• Effects last less time after you leave a storm or acid rain area.



You go prone automatically when knocked down

• If you try to change posture while knocked down, you try to stand up. You stand up automatically when the status ends.

• The prone @ is shown in gray in ASCII mode too.



Oct 01

Add pill that lets lactose intolerant characters eat milk and dairy for a short time without penalty



Add .50 machine gun security robot to central lab and military secret facility

• Aiming is very slow, but the laser sight warns you in advance.



More machine gun and grenade launcher turrets and security robots in the surface area of military secret facility

• The underground floors are deeper, so underground enemies cannot easily wait on the elevator.



Oct 02

Irradiated wanderer, charred nightmare, and private resort no longer drop old power armor



Add steel material to carbon fiber katar



Fix misplaced bed in Tacoma Commune barn



Add experimental fighter aircraft with a laser cannon

• Spawns with very low chance where other ground aircraft spawn.



Exporting a vehicle design also saves part shapes changed after installation



Sign text color is shown on the look around (x) screen



Oct 03



The filter rules stay visible in the window while you type a custom pickup zone filter



Fix ceiling lights going off in airports, malls, and labs without a power grid

• Replace the ceiling lights there with a type that works without power.



Add small park area with hell creatures that rarely spawns in cities

• It has lava and can have rare items.



Superglue and bone glue spawn in 250 mL squeeze tubes



Fix gasoline spilling on the floor when you buy it at a gas station



Add traits to alpha mutation that raise stamina, appearance, and move speed



Fix game exit when examining a bionic upgrade item



SWAT transport truck ambush sites are shown on the map as a light blue S

• Dead survivor sites are a red s. Vehicle sites are yellow.



Water cannon can fire lubricant and glue

• Portable water cannon tank capacity is 2 times the old value.



More pistols, revolvers, and pistol crossbows can use a pistol lanyard



Oct 04

The bigger the single hit, the stronger the bleeding

• Up to 3 levels of bleeding are applied per hit, based on damage / 25.



Improve leaking fuel puddle tiles on vehicles

• Adjacent fuel puddle tiles join smoothly.



Change advanced UPS charge cycle

• Changes from 40 charge every 12 minutes to 4 charge every minute.



Fix primitive weapon check of slingshot cannon, manual pump requirement for pneumatic shotgun crafting, and chain weapon wrap attack
