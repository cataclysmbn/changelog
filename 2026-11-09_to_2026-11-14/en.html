Bright Nights changelog: 2026-11-09 ~ 2026-11-14

Nov 9

Add ore smelter

Add an ore smelter that smelts while you do other tasks after you load it with ore and charcoal.

You can take the result after about 6 hours of operation.

▲ ore smelter in use



Add roof to The Tacoma Commune dismantling workshop

Add a roof to The Tacoma Commune dismantling workshop.

▲ dismantling workshop before and after



Increase battery charger output

Vehicle and power grid battery charger output increases from 15W to 60W.



Replace Medical mutation trait

Replace Narcoleptic with Sleepy in the Medical mutation line.



Show text in electronic device menus

E-book tablet functions for file access, game launching, and flashlight switching now show separately in the menu.



Nov 10

Fix dimension shift crash after death

Fix a crash when shifting to another dimension and deleting the current dimension right after an enemy kills you.



Translate clothing modification and item use text

Clothing modification menus and messages for item use, nanobots, and shouting now display in the selected language.



Nov 11

Expand tameable wild animals

Add several wild animals and fungal monsters that you can tame.

Most need traits from their line, and some can also be used as mounts.



Restore help shortcuts

Building and reloading shortcuts missing from 4 help screens show again, and custom shortcuts also apply.



Allow SMG conversion kits to be installed again

Fix an issue that prevented reinstalling the same conversion kit on an SMG after removing it.



Nov 12

Adjust forest animal spawns

Reduce the conditions that made forest animals disappear after early game, and simplify spawns by time of day.

Bear and crow cubs spawn in spring, fawns spawn in spring and summer, and some boar, dog, and cat spawns become young animals.

Wolves spawn in groups of 1~3 all day, and some animals do not spawn right after game start.



Fix lake and river saving and save lag

Fix an issue where newly generated lakes and rivers could be missing from saves.

Also fix severe lag while saving.



Improve The Tacoma Commune greenhouse

Add plant pots, water tanks, tables, shelves, and lights to The Tacoma Commune greenhouse.

▲ greenhouse after changes



Nov 13

Rework LMOE shelter

Change the LMOE shelter layout and add freezers and underground enemy spawns.

Normal shelter basements can have survivor zombies and ferals, but safe starting locations use special shelters without enemies.



Show taming requirements

Show food and required traits in the detailed description of enemies you can tame.

▲ detailed description showing taming requirements



Nov 14

Add lights to The Tacoma Commune shops and facilities

Add lights to The Tacoma Commune scout shop and replace lights in the tavern and clinic.



Add Crazy Cataclysm content

Add a new enemy to Crazy Cataclysm that runs away from the player.

It drops 3~5 rare, science, or military items when killed, and coyote packs also spawn in plains.
