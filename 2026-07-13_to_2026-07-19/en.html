Bright Nights changelog: 2026-07-13 ~ 2026-07-19

Jul 13

Add loopholes for plastered wooden walls

• Make a hole in a wall to see through it and shoot. Paint it to match the wall color or strip the paint.

• Ranged damage reduction of house walls and loopholes goes down from 20~40 to 8~16. Even .22 LR can now go through plaster walls.

• Fix building a wooden wall loophole giving two wood pieces only.



Jul 14

Normal, large, and collapsed office towers and office labs now spawn with multiple floors



Liquids can now be drawn from the vehicle menu into a container on the ground next to you



Adjust weight and material amounts of Arcana weapons and crafts

• The weight of shrike's misericorde goes down from 2 kg to 1.25 kg.

• Reduce the gold and silver materials that symbol of judgment and similar items needed in excess of the finished item.



Jul 15

Make the physics lab reports use metric units



Add a bionic that stops sunrise, heat sources, damage, and light from waking you while you sleep



Jul 16

Add a variant of the soldier corpse special location with a portal



Fix dismantling an acid juggernaut to give the same contents as other military acid zombies



Magical Nights soul armor now summons its own magical armor instead of plate armor

• Wear it over other gear with no encumbrance.

• Defense is lower, but it now gives ranged damage reduction, radiation resistance, and some protection from mental attacks.



Wrenches can now be found in mechanic garages and tool drawers



Fix vehicles near the Refugee Center spawning stuck in walls



Fix being able to try to reload or unload an advanced UPS



Expand the recipe search filter

• Search for reversible recipes, byproducts, melee damage, protection, warmth, storage, and encumbrance.

• Compare skill requirements and values, for example `s:electronics>=5`. Add `-` to exclude recipes that match.



Jul 17



Fix washing machines spawning stuck in the windows of city bars



Fix the Barry Isherwood rescue mission to point to the return point of the ground farm



Jul 18

Magical Nights soul armor, stoneskin, and frost armor can now be worn with all mutant body types

• Spell armor works even with a tail, hooves, claws, and similar parts.



Fix pet armor that could not be taken off

• Gear that is hard to put in your inventory, such as horse armor, can now be equipped from the ground nearby.



Blindness now applies only when a projectile hits the head or eyes

• Monsters with no eyes are immune to blindness. You do not go blind if a shield fully blocks the damage.

• Riot shields can now block acid projectiles.



The effects list now shows effects given by equipment enchantments, effects they block, and the mutations and traits they grant



Jul 19



Remove the "faulty" label before the name when you check or scan the bionics of a corpse



Fix a spawned .40 Kriss to load .40 ammo that fits its magazine



Reduce overlapping of sounds such as shoveling, thunder, and vehicle alarms during playback



Magical Nights weapon belts can now hold axes and golf clubs



Arcana refined blood offerings can now be used without holding them in your hands
