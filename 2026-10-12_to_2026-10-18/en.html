Bright Nights changelog: 2026-10-12 ~ 2026-10-18

Oct 12

Update the 2 mods bundled with the experimental build to the latest versions, with the latest translations and changes



Add wheelbarrow, step ladder, and tools to the Tacoma Commune tool shed



Wearing certain flags gives a morale bonus



Add carbon fiber armor plate and ram

• 33% more durability than the steel equivalent.

• Add projectile protection to military composite metal armor plate and ram.



C-4, C-4 breaching charge, and EMP bomb can set the explosion timer up to 10 turns, and small nuke up to 20 turns



The Tacoma Commune quest to collect 6 first aid kits accepts all 3 kinds of first aid kits



Add 150 ml storage to bra, and give it 1 encumbrance

• Volume of advanced e-cigarette and disposable e-cigarette decreases from 250 ml to 100 ml



Oct 13



Add roofs to Tacoma Commune sawmill, tool shed, and clinic



Fix trained animals losing training effects after saving

• Also fix horses that finished combat training stopping in front of enemies



Corn can be used to craft cow feed



Oct 14

Connect a microwave to power to use it as a cooking tool

• It can be used for food crafting that needs a multi cooker. A vehicle part version is also added.

• A powered microwave is placed in the camper van



Saddle bag capacity 30 L -> 50 L

• Encumbrance when worn directly also rises from 30 to 50, and crafting materials and required skills increase



Higher crafting difficulty for several large-capacity backpacks



hauler bot carry weight 180 kg -> 270 kg

• Health, speed, and protection also rise



20 gauge shot armor penetration 24 -> 12

• 20x66mm high-explosive: damage 16 -> 38, armor penetration 4 -> 13



Autodrive avoids craters, minefields, and road blocks

• Dangerous areas such as minefields are not put in the route unless you pick them directly as the destination



Oct 15

Sailboats can be steered directly without a start-up bypass

• No engine start message appears when you raise or lower the sail, and the pedals are removed from the wagon



Hovering vehicles no longer spawn on normal roads

• They appear only from military vehicle spawns and dedicated spawns



Door locks are no longer generated on sailboats, pirate ships, and similar vehicles



Oct 16



Add roof to Tacoma Commune scavenger shop, and remove broken windows



Grenade pouch and bandolier can hold 25mm ammo



Add perk menu

• Open it with Ctrl+P. You can see permanent and rare bonuses separately from traits



Foaming concrete slurry can be sprayed with the chemical sprayer

• It creates impassable terrain. A weak one is see-through and disappears after 12 hours



Auto-drink zones do not drink 2 kinds of stimulant drinks

• Prevents stimulant overdose



Oct 17

Rename small cargo ship terrain to river barge

• Reduces confusion with the sunken cargo ship



Big mouth billy bass decoration can be found in lake cabins, lighthouses, and hunting sites



Night vision goggles are worn on the belt layer instead of the normal worn layer

• Reduces unnecessary conflicts with other gear



Strengthen the hulk and juggernaut lines

• Kevlar hulk, zombie hulk, and relentless hulk gain grab, bite, strong swing, and other attacks, and some attacks deal armor-piercing damage

• skeletal juggernaut and fungal juggernaut also get stronger health, speed, melee, and special attacks



Oct 18



Add lighting, sink, cupboard, and cleaning supplies to the Tacoma Commune tavern

• A still appears after you finish the first bartender quest



Fix bonuses of traits you do not have being counted in the RPG System mod



Fix long strips of fake terrain tiles around the wild collapsed warehouse



zombie brute has a weaker smash, but gains more attack types such as shove and vehicle shove



Fix long-range melee attacks such as tentacles hitting through obstacles like vehicles
